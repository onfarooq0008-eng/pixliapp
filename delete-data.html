Delete your data

Pixli PDF Scanner. Last updated 23 October 2026.

Pixli has no accounts and no servers. There is no online account to delete and I hold no data about you. Everything lives on your phone, and you can remove it yourself.

Delete single files

Open My Files in the app, use the menu next to a file and choose Delete.

Delete everything the app stores

  • Open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then Pixli, then Storage, and choose Clear storage (or Clear data). The wording differs between phones.
  • Or uninstall the app. Files kept only inside the app are removed with it.

Copies outside the app

Copies saved to a folder you picked under Save to in Settings, and files you shared with other apps or cloud services, are not removed by the steps above. Delete them with your file manager or in those apps.

Temporary files

Photos taken while scanning are temporary and are cleared automatically the next time the app starts.
